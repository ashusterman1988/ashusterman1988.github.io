Agentic AI dashboard

Logistics Pulse

The problem:
logistics teams hear about port delays, strikes, and new rules late, and then have to work out what each one means for their customers.
What I built:
a live dashboard that pulls supply chain news and uses AI with web search to rate each story's impact on the business and say what to do about it.
What it shows:
an agentic AI tool taken from an idea to a deployed app, built around what a logistics team actually needs to act on.

  • Claude API with web search
  • Deployed on Vercel
